REEL 9 — "This tennis match was actually an interrogation"   (Series 2, part 3 · all EP10)

[HOOK]  ON SCREEN: THIS TENNIS MATCH WAS AN INTERROGATION 🎾
This isn't a tennis match. It's an interrogation.

[BEAT 1]  ON SCREEN: EVEN SMALL TALK IS A TRAP
L invites his number one suspect to play tennis. Even the small talk is dangerous.
L mentions he was the British junior champion. Light wonders: if he asks "are you British?",
will that look like Kira digging for L's identity? He asks anyway.
And L is ready for it: nothing there can reveal L's identity.

[BEAT 2]  ON SCREEN: WIN = SUSPICIOUS. LOSE = SUSPICIOUS.
Then the real trap. L knows Kira hates to lose. So if Light plays to win, he looks like Kira.
But if he loses on purpose, that looks like Kira hiding it. There is no safe move.
So Light decides the match can't prove anything... and plays to win.

[BEAT 3]  ON SCREEN: THEY'RE THINKING THE SAME THING 🧠
Every rally, both of them are thinking three moves ahead.
And they reach the exact same conclusion, at the exact same moment.
Light wins the match, six games to four.

[CLOSE]  ON SCREEN: WHO WON IT MENTALLY? 👇   (+ 1% suspicion meter)
Light won the match. But right after, L tells him to his face: "I suspect you are Kira."
Just one percent... So who won it mentally? Comment below.
