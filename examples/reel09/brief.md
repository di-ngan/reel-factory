# Reel 9 brief: "This tennis match was actually an interrogation"

All clips from EP10. Times are positions in the episode, checked against its subtitles. About 55 s.

## Clips
- **A** (1:49-2:10): L mentions he was the "British Junior Champion". Light wonders whether asking if he's British
  would look like Kira digging for L's identity, asks anyway ("Ryuga, were you raised in Britain?", 2:01.8), and L
  shuts it down: "There's nothing there that can reveal L's identity." (2:06.8)
- **B** (2:50-3:16): L's first serve. "The person who makes the first move wins."
- **C** (4:14-4:34): "Kira hates to lose, but..." (4:14.6) Light realizes losing on purpose is "also like Kira" (4:30.6).
- **D** (5:15-6:24): the dual monologue, ending on both thinking "We'll go to the task force headquarters!" (6:21.8)
- **E** (6:32-6:37): "Game set. Won by Yagami." / "Six games to four!" (6:36)
- **F** (7:05-7:25): L: "Yagami, I suspect that you, are Kira" (7:06.8) ... "only a one percent possibility" (7:23)
